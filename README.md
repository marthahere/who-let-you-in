**WHO LET YOU IN?**

Play here!: 

A browser-based investigation game about identity, digital footprints, and deciding what to trust online.

**About:**

In Who Let You In?, you’re a moderator for a fictional online community. People apply to join, and you investigate their backgrounds before deciding whether to let them in.

Some applicants are legitimate residents while others may be impersonating someone else or providing false information.

Each application brings together records, account histories, usernames, relationships, dates, and other traces left online.Some applicants are legitimate residents while others may be impersonating someone else or providing false information.

Your job is to look at the full picture. Keep track of what you find, connect clues across different records, and decide what the evidence actually tells you. You will then either accept or reject the applicant.

**Features:**

- Identity verification
- Digital footprints and account history
- Timeline and date-based investigations
- School and personal records
- Username history
- Check past online activity
- Phone connected people and relationships
- Notebook system for investigations
- Different investigation methods and case combinations

**Technologies:**

- HTML
- CSS
- JavaScript

A little bit about my process: Hi! I started working on Who Let You In? with the idea of making an identity-checking game around an online community. As the project developed, I focused on giving players more ways to investigate each application and weigh the evidence.

As I developed and played through the game, I kept thinking more about the player’s experience and what would make the investigations feel realistic. I’ve been refining the game mechanics with the goal of making each case feel distinct and giving players room to draw their own conclusions.
