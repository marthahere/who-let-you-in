**WHO LET YOU IN?**

A browser-based investigation game about identity verification, digital footprints and online community security.

**About:**

In Bellwood Investigation, the player works as a moderator for a fictional online community in the city of Bellwood.

Applicants request access to the community and the player must investigate their identity using different records and digital information. Some applicants are legitimate residents while others may be impersonating someone else or providing false information.

The player must review the evidence, take notes, connect information between people and records then decide whether to Accept or Reject each applicant.

**Features:**

- Identity verification
- Digital footprints and account history
- Timeline and date-based investigations
- School and personal records
- Username history
- Connected people and relationships
- Notebook system for investigations
- Procedurally generated visual identities
- Different investigation methods and case combinations

**Technologies:**
